Delete your Petwell account

This page explains how to delete your account and data in Petwell, the pet health app. You can do it at any time — in the app, or by email if you can't get into the app.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Petwell and sign in.
  2. To reach Settings, open the Records tab and tap the gear icon.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account & data.
  4. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

Deletion is permanent and starts right away. You are signed out, and the same sign-in no longer works.

If you can't access the app

Email support@petwell.app from the address on the account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm. We may ask you to reply from the account's email address, so that nobody else can delete your account.

What is deleted

Your account email, pet profiles, health logs, symptom sessions, scans and photos, records and documents, reports, reminders, Care Circle memberships, telehealth requests, purchases metadata held by Petwell, and AI history.

What is kept, and for how long

We do not keep a copy of your account data after deletion, apart from the following. Routine backups and service logs can persist for up to 30 days before they are purged. Crash reports are not linked to your account and follow our diagnostics provider's own retention schedule. Reference catalogs (food products, toxin references) are shared, non-personal data and are unaffected.

Apple and Google keep their own records of any purchase you made through them. Deleting your Petwell account does not cancel a store subscription — cancel that in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Delete some data without deleting your account

In Settings you can delete stored scan photos and delete your AI history while keeping your account.

Export first (optional, free)

Before deleting you can export everything as JSON from Settings → Export all my data.
